### A-F1 — Upstream monetary invariants are assumed rather than validated

**Rank: medium. Affected rows:** V2.2.1, V2.2.3.

**Evidence:** `backend/src/DisputeDesk.Infrastructure/CoreBanking/CoreBankingClient.cs:49–91,136–142` validates response shape and masks but not the monetary range, precision or supported currency. `backend/src/DisputeDesk.Domain/Disputes/Dispute.cs:373–374` explicitly assumes ZAR. An unexpected upstream amount or currency can therefore produce an invalid monetary combination, persistence failure or incorrect dispute amount. The current ZAR-only stub limits exposure but does not validate the service boundary.

**Proposal: fix.** Validate supported currency and documented monetary range/precision before accepting a transaction snapshot. Reject unsupported currency rather than implicitly combining it with a ZAR expected amount. Add malformed-upstream-response tests.

### A-F2 — Non-linking functions lack actor-bound abuse limits
