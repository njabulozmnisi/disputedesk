The ready-made program (scratchpad `smoke/smoke.cs`) calls the real `ICoreBankingClient` and `ISimSwapCheck` from DI.

- Stub up: match for D returned `CUS-000004` with the cellphone on record, page of 3 rows `TXN-D-0031, 0030, 0029` with next cursor, simSwap C fails, D passes, in 94 ms.
- Stub stopped (`docker compose stop core-banking`): match returned `core-banking-unavailable`, transactions returned `core-banking-unavailable`, both SIM-swap checks reported failed (fail closed), four calls in 1567 ms. Under the 5-second limit.
- Both named HttpClients report `Timeout = Infinite`; the deadline is enforced by whole-call cancellation, as the verifier noted. Circuit behaviour (5 calls in 30 s, 15 s open) is covered by `CoreBankingResilienceTests`, not re-driven here.
