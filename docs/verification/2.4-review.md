## Postmortem: second fix round (trigger)

- **What happened.** Fix item 2 asked for the "Not a card payment" hint on a settled debit "whose `notDisputableReason` is `notEligible`". The builder wrote it as the last line of a fall-through, after the pending, credit and own-account checks. In the same round the client was regenerated against 3.1's merged contract, which added `alreadyDisputed`, and nothing listed that value or required a test for it. A disputed card payment therefore says "Not a card payment" (M-new-1).
- **Why the stages missed it until the delta review.** The verifier checks criteria, and 2.4's criteria predate `alreadyDisputed`; the builder's tests covered the reasons it knew; the contract change entered mid-round through a rebase, not through a story.
- **Category.** Spec gap combined with a non-standard mechanism (fall-through instead of keying on the enum).
- **Countermeasure (deterministic).** The hint maps `notDisputableReason` with an exhaustive `switch` whose default is a `never` check, so any new enum value fails `tsc` until it is handled. For the supervisor: when a fix round regenerates a client against a changed contract, the brief names every new field and enum value and asks for a test per value.
