- The handler refuses a null, empty or whitespace `sub` before the lookup, then compares with `StringComparer.Ordinal`.
  Widening "null or empty" to whitespace is what makes the story's Inputs line ("an empty or whitespace element
  matches nothing") true; correct, and tested.
- AD-14a, one meaning of `ActorType` in code: the handler sets `Actor(ActorType.Operations, sub)` only for a listed
  `sub`; `RecordDeniedAsync` records `Operations` and the token's `sub` (empty when the token has none), described as
  the route's actor type. `Actor.cs` and `ActorType` carry no second meaning. Nothing else in `backend/src` assigns
  an operations actor.
- No null-forgiving `!`, no new suppression, no package, no openapi change (`openapi.json` unchanged after the build).
- The empty-list HTTP test uses `PostConfigure` after binding, the same technique as the removed empty-map test; the
  missing-list case is proved at the binding level. Together they cover "missing or empty".
