## Resolution

Sofia, 2026-10-30. 4.1 split into 4.1 and 4.2 (`docs/tickets/epic-4-events/tickets/`).

1. Fixed in 4.1 criterion 4 (ActivitySource `DisputeDesk` in `Telemetry.cs`, `AddSource`; `Telemetry.cs` in Touches). The stored pair is read through the propagator's `Extract` instead of `ActivityContext.TryParse` (security S2), injected with `default(Baggage)`.
2. Settled: 4.1 Build facts, Test setup (one xUnit collection with parallelisation off).
3. Fixed in 4.2 criterion 3 (its own Redpanda container); topic created with 3 partitions through the admin client in 4.1 Build facts, Test setup.
4. Fixed in 4.1 criterion 5 (`Kafka:BootstrapServers`, unreachable Postgres or broker, `WorkerHostTests` unedited); AD-26 already lists the key.
5. Fixed in 4.2 criterion 2 (fatal error recreates the producer, no attempt; builder verifies librdkafka guidance for 2.16.0, 4.2 Notes).
6. Fixed in 4.2 criteria 1, 4 and 5 (paused broker keeps its port, builder verifies the pause API) and 4.1 criterion 3 (per-host in-memory span exporter).
7. Fixed in 4.1 criterion 6 (idle wait at most 10 s, heartbeat after each send, heads one at a time) and 4.2 criterion 3 (1 s doubling, ±20% jitter, fake `TimeProvider`).
8. Fixed in 4.1 criterion 3 (short-lived `AppDbContext` on the lock connection, `UseNpgsql(connection)`).
9. Fixed in 4.1 criterion 1 (`SentAtUtc`, one clock read per batch, `"NextAttemptAtUtc" <= @now`).
10. Settled: 4.1 Build facts, Test setup and Packages (raise for version 1, hand-built later rows, Worker fixture with `MigrateAsync`, pins verified).
11. Settled: 4.1 Build facts, Shape (one `BackgroundService` plus `KafkaOptions`, private methods, `// shortcut:` for the index).
- Missing standards: `ActivityContext.TryParse` replaced by the propagator (4.1 criterion 4); `IMeterFactory` in 4.2 criterion 3; EF Core existing connection in 4.1 criterion 3.
