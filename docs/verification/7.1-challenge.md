## Resolution

Sofia, 2026-10-30. Every point was `[story]`; all are folded into the story, no `[decision]` raised.

- Customer D shared with `linking-verify.spec.ts`, and the undiscovered `e2e/journeys/`: the suite becomes two Playwright projects in the one config, `journeys` (`tests/journeys/**`) and `regression` (the rest), each run on its own fresh stack through `npm run journeys` and `npm test` (criterion 1). - The fixture row: `TXN-D-0030` (R849.00) and the pending `TXN-D-0031` for the edge case (Inputs, criterion 2).
- Accessibility coverage: the SPA screen states are named, including an error-summary state; axe runs at 1280px and 320px with a no-horizontal-scroll check for reflow; the hosted identity pages are scanned and recorded, not failed on (AD-6); the manual pass records what it checked per screen, and axe alone is not claimed as WCAG conformance (criteria 6 and 7).
- ZAP: target `http://host.docker.internal:8080`, image digest recorded, every alert triaged in `7.1-qa.md`, an untriaged High or Critical fails, and the unauthenticated passive scope is stated (criterion 8).
