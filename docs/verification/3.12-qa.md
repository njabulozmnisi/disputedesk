## Standards

- RFC 9110 and 9457: the 404 and 409 answers are `application/problem+json` with `type`, `status`, `traceId` and no exception text; a stranger's dispute answers the same as an unknown one (no existence oracle).
- RFC 9562 section 4: only the canonical GUID is sent; upper case is lower-cased before the request.
- OWASP ASVS V14.2.1: no dispute ID in `returnUrl`.
- WCAG 2.2: 2.4.3 focus order (dialog return, h1 after withdraw), 2.4.1 skip link on a direct load, 4.1.3 live region. axe (wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21a, wcag21aa, wcag22aa) reported no violation on: not-found, loading, loaded at 1280 and 320, failure, rejected at 1280 and 320, upheld, dialog, dialog with errors, withdrawn.
- Layout: at 1280 the summary and timeline are two columns (asserted from the boxes); at 320 there is no horizontal scroll (asserted).

## Style-src console violation (fixes item 8)

Confirmed: Radix's scroll-lock only. Probe: a `securitypolicyviolation` listener on the dispute detail while opening the dialog. Before opening: 0 violations. After: exactly 1, `style-src-elem`, blocked `inline`, from the app bundle, and the one console error is "Applying inline style violates ... style-src 'self'". The blocked element is a `<style>` holding `.with-scroll-bars-hidden` and `body[data-scroll-locked]` (react-remove-scroll-bar). Closing adds none. No other console error on any screen walked (the specs fail on any other). Consequence: the blocked rule is what hides the scrollbar and locks body scroll behind a dialog, so the page can still scroll behind it; cosmetic. The human decides between a CSP hash for that rule (the console message prints one) and accepting it. Not a 3.12 defect.

## Observations

- **O1 (pre-existing, not 3.12):** the unknown route (`/nothing-here`) renders the not-found screen outside the signed-in shell (no main navigation), while the not-found for a dispute ID has it. The screen itself (title, text, link) is identical, which is what the criterion states.
- **O2 (low, mockup):** `dispute-loading.html` is one full-width column; the screen shows the card and the 3 timeline placeholders in two columns (EXPERIENCE.md, Responsive, asks for two columns from 1024 px).
- **O3 (low, mockup):** the facts card differs from `dispute-rejected.html`: the mockup right-aligns values; the screen uses a left-aligned label and value grid. Content is the same.
- **O4 (low, mockup, decide):** timeline entries differ from `dispute-rejected.html` and `dispute-under-review.html`. The mockups put the sentence first, then actor and time, then the note in a grey quote box, on a rail with dots, with the status only on the resolve entry ("Resolved · Rejected. The bank didn't uphold your dispute."). The screen shows a bold status label line, the sentence, the note, then actor and time, on a plain rail. The screen follows the story's criterion 3 but not the mockup's order or quote box; the product owner should align one to the other.
- **O5 (low, mockup, decide):** the Rejected guidance is a muted paragraph with no heading or card; `dispute-rejected.html` shows a card headed "If you disagree with this outcome" with different wording. The screen uses the words the story fixes.
- **O6 (low, visual):** the withdraw dialog differs from `dispute-withdraw.html`: the title renders at label size in the heading colour (`text-heading font-strong` on `AlertDialogTitle`) where the mockup shows a larger dark title; the overlay is lighter and the dialog sits higher over the card. Behaviour is right.
- **O7 (info):** an unknown timeline `action` still renders an `li` holding only its time. The criterion (no line or label) holds; whether a bare timestamp should render is the product owner's call.
- Screenshot artefact: the 320 px full-page shots show the fixed phone tab bar across the page. It is a full-page capture effect, not a layout defect.
