## Observations (not blocking)

1. **Worker log volume during an outage:** 175 warning lines across the two broker stops (about 90 s in all), one per librdkafka reconnect failure. No data in them, and they are what the story asks for (one `[LoggerMessage]` warning, no record values). Story 4.2 may want to rate-limit them.
2. **Native `libgssapi_krb5.so.2` message** on the Worker's stderr at start (`Cannot load library libgssapi_krb5.so.2`). It is a GSSAPI probe by the Postgres driver or the runtime, not Kafka, and holds no data. The Api and the Worker both print it.
3. **Flaky, not 4.1:** `bff-sign-in-and-out.spec.ts:92` (logout leaves the session cookie in the browser context) failed once on the first full run on a cold stack and passed 3 of 3 reruns and in every later full run. The Api is unchanged by this story.
4. The relay can add up to about 10 s between the commit and the publish (one trace showed the Worker span 7.8 s after the raise); within the story's idle bound.
