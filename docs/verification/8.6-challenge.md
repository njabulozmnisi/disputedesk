## Standards
- [story] A zero-count `expect.poll` is not a bounded absence check. Use Playwright’s [retrying assertions](https://playwright.dev/docs/test-assertions) for eventual positive conditions and [request/response events](https://playwright.dev/docs/api/class-page#page-wait-for-request) for observable work. If the requirement truly means “nothing occurs for 500–700 ms,” retain an explicit bounded observation window rather than disguising it as a signal-based poll; Playwright [discourages arbitrary timeout waits](https://playwright.dev/docs/api/class-page#page-wait-for-timeout), not the passage of time inherent in that claim.
- [story] No other proposed mechanism needs a new custom standard; the shared helpers and Playwright lint rule follow the sources cited in the story.
