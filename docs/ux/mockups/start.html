Something wrong with a card transaction?

Dispute it online in a few steps, then follow its status on one timeline until the bank resolves it.

How it works

  1. Link your login

    Create a login, then prove you're a customer of the bank with your ID number, account number and a one-time code sent to your cellphone.

  2. Pick a card transaction

    Choose a card transaction from the last 90 days, say what's wrong and add a note if it helps.

  3. Follow its status

    Each change the bank makes shows on your dispute's timeline, with any note meant for you.
