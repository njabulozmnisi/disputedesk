# DisputeDesk: experience spine

## Foundation

A single responsive web SPA, mobile-first: designed at 360px, scaling to 1280px desktop. React and TypeScript, shadcn/ui on Tailwind (Radix primitives supply focus management, roles and keyboard support), TanStack Query hooks generated by Orval from `openapi.json`. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) is the visual identity; this file is how the product behaves. Where shadcn or Radix already defines a behaviour (dialog focus trap, radio-group arrow keys, toast announcement), it is inherited and not restated.

Sign-in and sign-up are hosted by the identity provider and themed from the same tokens (DESIGN.md, *Theme file and hosted-login theming*). DisputeDesk designs no authentication logic: it sends the person to the hosted pages and receives them back through the BFF.

The SPA never decides who may see what. Every rule here (which rows show Dispute, when Withdraw appears) is a presentation of a decision the API also enforces; hiding a control is cosmetic.

## Information Architecture

| # | Surface | Route | Reached from | Authorisation state | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Start page | `/` | Direct visit, sign-out | anonymous | One sentence on what DisputeDesk does; Sign in and Create a login; a sample dispute card; How it works in three steps |
| 1 | Sign in / sign up | identity provider | Start page buttons; session expired | anonymous | Hosted, themed; email verification happens here |
| 2a | Link your login: details | `/link` | First return from sign-in while unlinked; any 403 "linking required" | unlinked | ID number and account number |
| 2b | Link your login: OTP | `/link/otp` | After 2a | unlinked | Enter the 6-digit OTP |
| 3 | Transactions | `/transactions` | After linking; nav; default for linked customers | linked | Newest first, Load more, status or disputability on each row |
| 4a | Raise a dispute | `/transactions/:transactionId/dispute` | Dispute on a row | linked | Reason, note, expected amount (wrong amount only) |
| 4b | Review and submit | `/transactions/:transactionId/dispute/review` | Continue on 4a | linked | Read back, then Submit dispute |
| 5 | Disputes | `/disputes` | Nav | linked | Open and closed disputes, newest first |
| 6 | Dispute detail | `/disputes/:disputeId` | A dispute row; a status badge on a transaction row; after submit | linked | Summary, timeline, Withdraw where allowed, guidance after Rejected |
| — | Session ended | `/signed-out?reason=expired` | Any 401 while signed in | anonymous | Explain, Sign in again |
| — | Not found | any unknown route; 404 on a dispute | — | any | Same screen for unknown and not-yours |

Navigation: two destinations only, **Transactions** and **Disputes**, plus **Sign out**. Phone: bottom tab bar. From 768px: top bar. The linking screens show the wordmark and Sign out only, because nothing else is reachable while unlinked.

Routing by authorisation state: a linked customer opening `/link` is sent to `/transactions` (403 "already linked"); an unlinked login opening any customer route is sent to `/link` (403 "linking required"). After sign-in the SPA returns to the route the person asked for, accepting only same-origin relative paths.

Mockups: [mockups/index.html](mockups/index.html) lists every screen and state. This spine wins on any conflict with a mockup.

## Voice and Tone

Plain, calm, short. Second person. Say what happened and what to do next. Use the glossary terms exactly ([../glossary.md](../glossary.md)); never their listed synonyms.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Dispute this transaction" | "File a claim", "Raise a ticket", "Chargeback" |
| "Your login" (the sign-in identity) | "Your account" for the sign-in identity (account means a bank account) |
| "Link your login" / "Linking" | "Enrol", "Register", "Onboard", "Verify your profile" |
| "Enter your OTP, the 6-digit one-time code we sent" | "Enter your PIN", "verification code" |
| "Status: Under review" | "State", "Stage" |
| "Outcome: Upheld" (with "The bank agreed with you") | "Decision", "Verdict" |
| "Withdraw this dispute" | "Cancel claim", "Close case" |
| "The bank" as the actor on the timeline | Any person's or tool's name |
| "We'll update the timeline as your dispute moves." | "We'll resolve this within 10 days." Any time promise or due date |
| "Something went wrong on our side. Try again." | "Error 503: upstream dependency failure" |

**Never shown to a customer:** an SLA, a due date, an expected resolution time, an actor's identity, the word "Escalated" (always "Under review (prioritised)"), whether linking details matched.

## Component Patterns

Behavioural rules. Visual specs are in DESIGN.md, *Components*.

| Component | Where | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction row | Transactions | Not a link as a whole. Its single action is the trailing control: "Dispute" (to 4a), the status badge (to 6), or nothing for not-disputable rows. The hint is plain text, read with the row. |
| Load more | Transactions, Disputes | Button below the list, fetches the next keyset page (20). Disappears when there is no next cursor. After loading, focus stays on the button; new rows are announced ("20 more transactions loaded"). No infinite scroll. |
| Reason card group | 4a | Radix `RadioGroup`: one tab stop, arrow keys move. Selecting Wrong amount reveals the Expected amount field directly below the group; selecting any other reason hides it and clears its value. |
| Note field | 4a, withdraw dialog | Optional. Live count "312 / 1,000" (500 for withdraw), counted after trimming, announced politely only at 900 and at the limit. Input past the limit is blocked. |
| Expected amount | 4a (wrong amount only) | Text input, `inputmode="decimal"`, "R" prefix. Validates on blur and Continue: required, at least R0.00, below the transaction amount, at most 2 decimals. Shows the disputed difference live underneath: "You're disputing R500.00". |
| Review card | 4b | Reads back transaction, reason, note, disputed amount. "Change" returns to 4a with values kept. No re-entry of anything already given. |
| Submit dispute | 4b | See *Idempotent submit* below. |
| Status badge | Rows, detail header | Text is the customer-facing status. On a transaction row it links to the dispute. |
| Timeline | 6 | Oldest first, as the API returns it. Each entry: status line, actor label "You" or "The bank", time in SAST, customer-visible note if any. Ordered list semantics. |
| Withdraw | 6 | Shown only for Submitted, Under review, Under review (prioritised). Secondary button opens an `AlertDialog`: optional note (500), "Withdraw dispute" (destructive) and "Keep dispute". On success: dialog closes, toast "Dispute withdrawn", timeline gains the entry. |
| Problem notice | Any error | Plain sentence, next step, and "Reference: {traceId}" with Copy. Copy confirms with "Copied" for 2 seconds. |
| OTP input | 2b | One input, six visual cells. Accepts paste and the platform's one-time-code autofill. Auto-submits when the sixth digit arrives; Verify button stays available. |

## State Patterns

| State | Surface | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Loading (first load) | Transactions, Disputes, Detail | `Skeleton` rows matching the layout (5 rows; detail: header plus 3 timeline entries). Page title renders immediately. `aria-busy` on the list. |
| Loading (more) | Load more | Button shows a spinner and "Loading…", stays focusable, is not clickable twice. |
| Empty | Transactions | "No transactions in the last 90 days." then "Card and account transactions appear here once they reach your account. Disputes you raised earlier are still in Disputes." (Disputes is a link). No button. |
| Empty | Disputes | "You haven't disputed any transactions." then "If something on your account looks wrong, find it in your transactions and choose Dispute. It will show here with its status." Secondary button "Go to transactions". |
| Empty section | Disputes | Open or Closed section with no items is hidden; if both empty, the empty state above. |
| Core banking unavailable (503) | Transactions | Problem notice: "We can't load your transactions right now. Your disputes are still available." Buttons: "Try again" (primary), "View disputes". |
| Field errors (400) | 2a, 4a, withdraw | Inline under the field, plus an error summary at the top of the form that links to each field; focus moves to the summary. The ID number is never echoed back. |
| Already disputed (409) | 4b submit | Notice: "This transaction already has a dispute. You can only dispute a transaction once." Link "View dispute" (the transactions list is refetched). |
| No longer disputable (422) | 4b submit | Notice: "This transaction can't be disputed any more." plus the API's reason (e.g. "It's now older than 90 days"). Button "Back to transactions"; the row refreshes. |
| Dispute not found (404) | 6 | Full screen "We can't find that dispute." Button "Go to your disputes". Identical for unknown IDs and other customers' disputes. |
| Unexpected error (5xx, network) | Any | Problem notice with "Try again" and the reference to quote to the bank. |
| Rate limited (429) | 2a, 2b | "Too many attempts. Try again in {n} minutes." from `Retry-After`, rounded up. Form disabled until then. |
| Linking lockout | 2b | See *Linking without disclosure*. |
| Session ended (401) | Any | Full screen: "You've been signed out. For your security, we sign you out after 15 minutes without activity." Button "Sign in again" returns to the same route afterwards. ### Problem-type mapping

The SPA routes on ProblemDetails `type`, never on message text.

| HTTP | Problem | SPA behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | any | Session ended screen |
| 403 | linking required | Redirect to `/link` |
| 403 | already linked | Redirect to `/transactions` |
| 404 | not found | Not found screen (detail) or "no longer available" notice (raise) |
| 409 | already disputed / not allowed in current status / idempotency in progress | Per state table; "in progress" keeps the spinner and retries the same request after 1 second, up to 5 times |
| 422 | not disputable / idempotency mismatch | Not-disputable notice; mismatch is shown as an unexpected error (it means a bug) |
| 429 | rate limited | Rate-limited state |
| 503 | core banking unavailable | Retry notice |

TanStack Query: no automatic retry on 4xx; one retry on 503 and network errors for reads, none for writes. After a successful raise or withdraw, the transactions and disputes queries are invalidated.

## Interaction Primitives

**Idempotent submit.** When the review screen opens, the SPA creates one `Idempotency-Key` (a random UUID) for that draft. Every attempt to submit that draft sends the same key; changing the draft (via Change) creates a new key. While the request is in flight, Submit shows "Submitting…" and ignores further presses. If the network drops before a response, the notice says: "We couldn't confirm your dispute was received. Try again; you won't create a second dispute." The retry reuses the key, so a replay returns the original 201.

**Back and forward.** Browser Back from 4b returns to 4a with values kept (held in memory for the session, not in storage). After a successful submit, Back from the detail goes to Transactions, not to the review screen.

**Keyboard.** Every action is reachable by Tab in reading order. Enter submits forms. Esc closes the withdraw dialog (Radix). No custom shortcuts.

**Touch.** 44px minimum targets. No hover-only affordances, no swipe actions, no drag.

**Banned:** infinite scroll, auto-advancing carousels, time-limited screens other than the OTP's own 5-minute validity, any countdown implying a resolution time.

## Linking without disclosure

Linking must never reveal whether a person banks here, whether their details matched, or why a code failed.

1. **Details (2a).** ID number (13 digits, `inputmode="numeric"`, `autocomplete="off"`) and account number. Format errors (length, ID checksum) are caught client-side and by the API's 400, naming the field only: "Enter a valid 13-digit ID number." After submit, the response is always the same.
2. **Neutral message (2b, top).** Always: "If these details match our records, we've sent a code to the cellphone number we hold for you." No masked number is shown, since showing one would confirm a match.
3. **OTP (2b).** "Didn't get a code? Start again" (back to 2a, which invalidates the earlier challenge, subject to rate limits). The code is valid for 5 minutes; the screen says "The code expires 5 minutes after we send it" without a live countdown.
4. **Wrong code.** "That code didn't work. You have {n} tries left." Shown identically for a wrong code, an expired code, and a login with no active challenge (no match, already linked, or failed SIM-swap check), because the API answers all of them as a wrong code [ASSUMPTION: showing tries left is acceptable].
5. **Lockout (after 3).** One screen for every case, including the SIM-swap failure: heading "We can't link you online", body "Please contact the bank. If you think you mistyped your details or the code, you can start again." Secondary button "Start again". Showing different copy for the SIM-swap case would leak the check's result, so the FR-6 copy is used for all lockouts [ASSUMPTION: Njabulo confirms merging the two states].
6. **Rate limited.** The 429 state above. Copy does not distinguish per-login and per-IP limits.
7. **Success.** Straight to Transactions with a toast "Your login is linked." No new sign-in.

## Accessibility Floor

Behavioural. Visual contrast is in DESIGN.md, *Contrast*. The PRD has no accessibility NFR; this spine sets the floor at **WCAG 2.2 AA** [ASSUMPTION: Njabulo adds this as an NFR].

- Every screen has one `h1` (the title) and landmark regions (`header`, `nav`, `main`). Route changes move focus to the `h1` and update `document.title` ("Transactions · DisputeDesk").
- Status is never conveyed by colour alone: badge text and icon always. Not-disputable rows say why in text.
- Forms: visible labels (no placeholder-only fields), errors tied with `aria-describedby`, an error summary that takes focus, required fields marked in text.
- Async results are announced through one polite live region: "Dispute submitted", "20 more transactions loaded", "Dispute withdrawn". Errors use `role="alert"`.
- **2.2 specifics:** focus is never hidden under the phone bottom bar (`scroll-padding-bottom` equals its height); targets are at least 24px (we use 44px); linking is an accessible authentication flow (paste and one-time-code autofill allowed, no puzzle); the review step never asks again for what was entered (redundant entry); "Contact the bank" guidance sits in the same place on every linking state (consistent help); no dragging anywhere.
- Amounts are read as words where needed: a debit shows as "R849.00" and a credit as "+R1 200.00"; each carries an accessible label ("R849.00 out", "R1 200.00 in"); masked numbers are labelled "card ending 3456".
- Times show "SAST" and carry a `<time datetime>` in ISO 8601 UTC.
- Works at 200% zoom and at 320 CSS px reflow without horizontal scrolling. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (no motion beyond 150ms fades anyway) and `prefers-color-scheme`.
- Tested with keyboard only, VoiceOver on iOS Safari, and TalkBack on Android Chrome [manual check]; axe checks run in the Playwright suite.

## Inspiration & Anti-patterns

- **Taken from banking app conventions in general:** newest-first transaction list with merchant left and amount right; masked card and account numbers; a status pill per item; one decision per screen in money-moving flows; a review step before anything is sent.
- **Taken from good government-service design:** error summaries that link to fields, plain-language explanations of why something isn't possible, no dead ends (every error says what to do next).
- **Rejected: chat or wizard bots for disputes.** A six-option form is faster and clearer.
- **Rejected: promises of time.** No "usually resolved in X days", no progress bars implying a schedule; the SLA stays internal.
- **Rejected: red alarm styling for disputes.** A dispute is routine; only errors and a Rejected outcome use the danger tone.
- **Rejected: showing the masked cellphone in linking.** It would confirm the details matched.

## Key Flows

Journey names follow PRD §2.4.

### UJ-1: Thandi signs up, links and disputes a transaction she doesn't recognise

Thandi, a teacher in Durban, on her phone at break, sees an R849.00 debit from "ZXP*GLOBALSVCS" she has never heard of.

1. On the start page she taps **Create a login**. The hosted sign-up page carries DisputeDesk's wordmark and cobalt button. She verifies her email.
2. Back in DisputeDesk she lands on **Link your login**: ID number, account number, **Continue**.
3. She reads: "If these details match our records, we've sent a code to the cellphone number we hold for you." Her phone offers the code from the SMS; she taps it, the six cells fill, and it submits.
4. Toast "Your login is linked." She is on **Transactions**, newest first. The R849.00 row has a **Dispute** link; the pending fuel row above it says "Pending. You can dispute it once it has settled."
5. She taps **Dispute**, picks **Unrecognised**, writes "I've never bought from this merchant", taps **Continue**, checks the review card (Disputed amount R849.00) and taps **Submit dispute**.
6. **Climax:** the dispute detail opens with "Dispute submitted" and a timeline of one entry: Submitted, You, 26 Oct 2026, 09:14 SAST. Back on Transactions the R849.00 row now shows the **Submitted** badge instead of Dispute. Under two minutes, no phone call.
7. She taps **Sign out**; both sessions end and she is on the start page.

Failure: her signal drops on Submit. The notice says she won't create a second dispute; **Try again** replays the same key and lands on the same detail.

### UJ-2: Sipho withdraws a dispute he no longer needs

1. Sipho opens **Disputes**, sees "TechNova Online, Not received, Under review" under Open, and opens it.
2. He taps **Withdraw dispute**. The dialog explains "You can't dispute this transaction again after you withdraw." He types "Parcel arrived" and confirms.
3. **Climax:** the badge changes to **Withdrawn**, the timeline ends "You withdrew this dispute. 'Parcel arrived'", the Withdraw button is gone, and the dispute moves to Closed.

### UJ-4 as seen by a customer: a dispute is prioritised

Lindiwe opens her Online Gadgets dispute. The timeline shows Submitted (You), Under review (The bank), then **Under review (prioritised)** (The bank) with no reason given and no date promised. She can still withdraw.

### UJ-3 as seen by a customer: an outcome arrives

The detail shows **Resolved · Upheld**, "The bank agreed with you", and the bank's note "We've reversed this debit." For **Resolved · Rejected**, the bank's note is followed by the complaint and National Financial Ombud Scheme guidance. Neither offers Withdraw.

### UJ-5 unhappy paths in the SPA

- **(a)** A pasted link to another customer's dispute shows "We can't find that dispute," the same as a mistyped ID.
- **(b)** A resolved dispute never shows Withdraw.
- **(c)** Three wrong codes end on "We can't link you online" with **Start again**.
- **(d)** An unlinked login opening `/transactions` is taken to **Link your login**.
- **(e)** The row of a transaction whose dispute was Rejected shows "Resolved · Rejected" and no Dispute control; a stale tab that submits anyway gets the already-disputed notice.
