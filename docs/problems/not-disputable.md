# Not disputable

- Type: `https://github.com/njabulozmnisi/disputedesk/blob/main/docs/problems/not-disputable.md`
- Status: 422

## When it happens

The transaction cannot be disputed. The `reason` extension says why: not eligible (pending, a credit, a transfer between own accounts) or outside the 90-day window.

## What a client should do

Do not retry. Explain the reason to the person.
